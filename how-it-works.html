Process Overview

Property Monitoring Made Simple

A transparent step-by-step customer journey from registration to continuous reporting.

01

STEP 1 — Register Your Property

Provide your contact details, property location and basic property information.

02

STEP 2 — Choose Your Service Package

Our team helps you understand the available monitoring options and select a suitable package.

03

STEP 3 — Property Onboarding

Once enrolment and payment are confirmed, your property is activated under the applicable service plan.

04

STEP 4 — Scheduled Inspection

Our field team visits the property according to the inspection frequency applicable to your package.

05

STEP 5 — Digital Updates

Applicable inspection photographs, observations and reports are made available through the V Secure platform/app.

06

STEP 6 — Issue Identification

If an issue is identified during an inspection, you are informed and suitable maintenance or support options may be recommended.

07

STEP 7 — Additional Support

Request maintenance, fencing, documentation, valuation, resale or other eligible services whenever required.
