Operational Workflow

Property Monitoring Made Simple

From instant online onboarding to verifiable digital inspection reports — follow the 7-step customer journey.

1

Register Your Property

Provide your contact details, plot dimensions, layout approval number, and precise geographic location. You can sign up via our online form or speak directly with our onboarding specialists.

2

Choose Your Service Package

Select between Standard Care (Quarterly inspections) or Premium Care (Monthly inspections). If you own multiple plots in the same vicinity, we configure a volume-discounted custom plan.

3

Property Onboarding & Verification

Once enrolment is confirmed, your property profile is created, coordinates are geo-mapped, and an assigned field officer reviews boundary survey documents.

4

Scheduled Field Inspections

Our uniformed officers visit the site on schedule. They inspect corner demarcation pegs, examine perimeter fencing, and check for unauthorized dumping or activity.

5

Geo-Tagged Digital Updates

Applicable inspection photographs with embedded GPS timestamps and detailed field observations are compiled and uploaded directly to your online account.

6

Issue Identification & Alerts

If overgrown wild shrubs, broken boundary stones, or potential neighbor encroachment are observed, you receive immediate priority notifications along with photos.

7

On-Demand Maintenance Coordination

Request plot weed clearing, barbed wire fencing, compound wall building, EB connection, or Patta assistance at pre-negotiated transparent contractor rates.
