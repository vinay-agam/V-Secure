Our Services

Everything Your Property Needs. In One Place.

V Secure goes beyond routine site visits by offering a range of property monitoring and support services.

01

Property Monitoring & Inspection

Professional property inspections help you stay informed about the physical condition of your land. Depending on the selected package, our team conducts scheduled inspections and documents relevant observations.

Property condition checks Boundary observations Visible issue identification Photographic documentation Inspection updates
02

Photo, Video & Geo-Tagged Updates

See what's happening without being there. Applicable property visits are documented through photographs and inspection updates, helping you maintain a digital record of your property.

Customers can access applicable property updates through their V Secure account/app.

03

Boundary & Encroachment Monitoring

Property boundaries are observed during scheduled inspections. If potential encroachment concerns or visible boundary issues are identified, the customer is informed so that appropriate action can be considered.

Monitoring is observational and does not replace a professional land survey or legal opinion.
04

Plot Cleaning & Maintenance

We can coordinate additional maintenance requirements based on the property's needs.

Plot Cleaning Vegetation Clearance Fencing Compound Wall Borewell EB Connection
05

Market Valuation Report

Understand the current market context of your property. V Secure can provide a market valuation report based on prevailing market conditions and recent relevant property transactions.

06

Property Documentation Assistance

Get assistance coordinating property-related documentation requirements.

EC Verification Patta Assistance Property Records Other Administrative Requirements
07

Property Resale Assistance

Planning to sell your property? V Secure can provide property resale assistance as an additional service based on customer requirements.

08

Property-Related Legal Support

V Secure can coordinate property-related legal support based on the customer's specific requirements.

Legal advice, where required, should be provided by appropriately qualified professionals.
