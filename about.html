A Division of VTrust Realty Pvt. Ltd.

V Secure Property Services is a division of VTrust Realty Pvt. Ltd., created to help property owners professionally monitor and manage their real estate assets.

Owning land is an important investment. But when you live away from your property or cannot visit it regularly, keeping track of its physical condition and maintenance requirements can become difficult.

V Secure bridges this gap through a combination of professional field inspections, digital documentation, property maintenance coordination and customer support.
